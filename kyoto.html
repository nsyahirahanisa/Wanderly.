🇯🇵 Grand Japan All-In Tour

A premium, hassle-free journey across Japan's golden route. From the serene bamboo forests of Kyoto to the neon skylines of Tokyo, experience the perfect blend of tradition, luxury onsen retreats, and modern city life.

Kyoto & Mie
  • Arashiyama: Iconic Sagano Bamboo Grove walk.
  • Kiyomizudera: Historic wooden stage with city views.
  • Iga-ryu Ninja: Explore the heritage of Mie coastline.
Fuji & Tokyo
  • Oshino Hakkai: Clear ponds from Mt. Fuji snowmelt.
  • Onsen Experience: Traditional hot spring stay.
  • Asakusa & Shinjuku: Ancient temples and modern neon streets.

Arrival and transfer to Kyoto. Experience Kiyomizudera Temple and the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Enjoy traditional Japanese meals included in the itinerary.

Discover Mikimoto Pearl Island in Mie before heading to the Fuji area. Check-in to a Traditional Onsen Resort. Visit the sacred Oshino Hakkai ponds.

Experience the neon lights of Shinjuku and the luxury of Ginza. Visit Sensoji Temple in Asakusa before your final transfer to the airport for your flight back to KL.
✈️ Transport & Guide
  • Round-trip economy flights from KL included.
  • Professional tour bus for all sightseeing.
  • Mandarin/English-speaking professional guide.
🏨 Stay & Meals
  • 5 nights: Premium hotels + 1 Night Onsen Resort.
  • All Main Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, & Dinner included.
❌ Exclude
  • Tipping for guide and driver.
  • Travel Insurance (Strongly Recommended).
  • Personal expenses & souvenirs.
🛍 Shopping Spots
  • Ginza (Luxury) & Shinjuku (Electronics/Fashion).
  • Nakamise-dori (Traditional Crafts).
