❄️ Kyushu Winter Rail Adventure

Journey through the scenic winter landscapes of Southern Japan using the iconic JR Kyushu Rail system. From the volcanic caldera of Mount Aso to the "Hells" of Beppu, this tour combines history, nature, and the ultimate onsen relaxation.

History & Castles
  • Nagasaki: Peace Park & Atomic Bomb Museum.
  • Kumamoto Castle: One of Japan's most resilient fortresses.
  • Fukuoka: Vibrant city life and canal-side dining.
Nature & Onsens
  • Mount Aso: Majestic volcanic winter caldera views.
  • Beppu Hells: Tour the geothermal "Umi Jigoku" (Sea Hell).
  • Yufuin: A fairy-tale town by Lake Kinrin.

Arrive in Fukuoka and activate your JR Kyushu Rail Pass. Head to Nagasaki for a deep dive into history at the Peace Park. Return to Kumamoto to witness the grand Kumamoto Castle.

Scenic train ride to Mount Aso. Experience the geothermal wonders of Beppu's "Hells". Stay overnight in a Traditional Ryokan with a hot spring bath.

Stroll through the charming boutiques of Yufuin. End your trip with a shopping spree at Tenjin and Canal City in Fukuoka before departure.
✈️ Transport & Rail
  • Return economy flights (KL ⇄ Fukuoka).
  • JR Kyushu Rail Pass included for all train travels.
  • Airport to hotel transfers in Japan included.
🏨 Stay
  • 6 nights in 3★–4★ hotels (Fukuoka, Nagasaki, Kumamoto).
  • 1 Night Ryokan/Onsen Hotel stay included.
🍜 Winter Specialties (Must Try)
  • Hakata Tonkotsu Ramen & Nagasaki Champon.
  • Basashi (Horse Meat) & Kumamoto Akaushi Beef.
  • Seasonal Amaou Strawberries.
🛍 Shopping Spots
  • Canal City (Fukuoka) & JR Hakata City.
  • Yufuin Floral Village (Local Crafts).

Perfect for train enthusiasts and winter scenery lovers.
