🗓 Highlights & Activities

Water Adventures
  • White Water Rafting: Grade 1–3 rapids, beginner friendly and thrilling.
  • Waterfall Abseiling: Challenge yourself descending waterfalls.
  • River Tubing: Relaxing drift down the river.
Land & Night Vibes
  • Hiking Batu Putih: Scenic trails with rewarding views.
  • Campfire & BBQ: Bonding sessions under the stars at the glampsite.

📌 Best for: Adventure seekers, team building, friends getaway, and youth groups.

Self-drive to Himalaya Camp, Perak. Check-in to your Glamp (Everest/Gokyo/Lothse). Afternoon River Tubing session followed by a cozy dinner at camp.

Morning White Water Rafting (Grade 1-3). Lunch box provided. Afternoon Waterfall Abseiling session. Evening BBQ Night with campfire experience.

Early morning Hiking at Batu Putih for sunrise/views. Final breakfast at camp before checking out.
